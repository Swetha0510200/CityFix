How CityFix Works

A transparent 4-step redressal pipeline connecting citizens to city municipal crews.

Step 1
1. Report

Snap a photo of the civic defect, select the category, and auto-detect your GPS location.

Step 2
2. Review

Municipal admin reviews the complaint, verifies severity, and assigns the work order to field crews.

Step 3
3. Resolve

Repair crews fix the issue on-site and the administrator posts official resolution remarks.

Step 4
4. Track

Citizens track the live resolution status timeline anytime using their unique Complaint ID.
